# Change 1: Homepage refresh

## Goals

- Keep the site light, simple to follow, readable, and visually pleasing without unnecessary decoration.
- Reduce the hero headline by roughly 25–30% and change it to **“Hi, I'm Mary”**.
- Make the introduction more personal and specific:

  > I’m a data-driven problem solver who loves figuring out how things can work better.  
  > My background is in analytics and operations, and I’m currently pursuing my MBA at Berkeley Haas with a focus on strategy and product.

- Remove the corporate-sounding hero eyebrow rather than adding another label.
- Replace the redundant **About me** and **View experience** buttons with one primary **See my work →** button and a quieter **About me** text link.
- Use **Work** instead of **Experience** in the navigation and portfolio labels.
- Use the right side of the hero for the introduction so the layout feels balanced; stack the content on smaller screens.

## Implemented result

These homepage goals were already implemented and published before this record was added. This branch starts from the current published site and adds this change log without repeating or rewriting the existing homepage commit.
